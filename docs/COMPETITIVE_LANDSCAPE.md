their App Store listings, not hands-on product verification. "Not listed" means
that the source did not establish a feature, not that it definitely does not
exist. Ankur's local beta is not yet a production release.

## What this means for Ankur

1. Parent/nanny access, photos, daily logs, invite codes and AI are not unique by
   themselves. Daily Nanny already advertises AI day summaries; Nestly advertises
   a grocery list with nanny access. Do not position Ankur as the first to do
   either.
2. A plausible positioning hypothesis is a **caregiver-to-parent handoff linked
   to household action**: the day's observations feed the next routine,
   parent decisions and shared shopping/tasks, rather than accumulating only
   activity records. This is an inference from the reviewed feature pages, not
   proof of an unserved market or a durable advantage.
3. Test this hypothesis with actual parent/caregiver pairs. Observe whether
   both people complete a daily handoff, whether the parent takes action from
   it, whether shopping/routine changes stay in the app, and whether the
   caregiver prefers it to their current chat and tracker workflow.
4. The app currently has a working local share-code join and local photo flow.
   AI has a quota migration but is disabled in the local rehearsal; email-link
   invites and a production release remain pending. Avoid marketing roadmap
   capabilities as already shipped.
